Mess Rules

  • Always carry your mess card to enter the mess hall.
  • Wasting food is strictly prohibited. Please take only as much as you can eat.
  • Maintain cleanliness in the mess area.
  • Mess timings should be strictly followed; late entries will not be entertained.
  • No outside food is allowed in the mess premises.
